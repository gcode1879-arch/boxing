Cam Boxing

Stand back so your head, shoulders and arms are visible. Your character copies your arms: raise your right hand and he raises his right arm.

Punch by thrusting your fist toward the camera. Block by keeping your fists up near your face, not pointed at the opponent. Guard with both hands to take almost no damage.

Elbow strikes count too, but only when he steps in close. Click the camera preview to make it bigger.

No camera? Keyboard works too: Z / X punch, C to guard.
