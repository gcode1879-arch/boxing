How to play

  • Straight punch: throw it forward
  • Hook: swing your fist sideways
  • Uppercut: swing your fist upward
  • Block: both fists up by your face
  • Hit the opponent while he winds up for a counter bonus
  • Keyboard: Z X punch, C hook, V uppercut, hold Space block
